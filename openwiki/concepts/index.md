# Files

- [CLI Output, Progress & Prompts](cli-output-progress-and-prompts.md) - The user-feedback layer — the global indicatif MultiProgress and its three bar styles, run_blocking as the bridge that turns a format crate's &mut dyn FnMut(u64) progress callback into bar increments (now including CHD via chd-rs), the print_* helpers that render every line to stderr, the indicatif-log-bridge, and the dialoguer prompts with their -y/--yes, --unattended, --system/--rom and cfg!(test) bypasses that keep headless runs (and the MCP stdout JSON-RPC channel) unblocked.
- [File Type Detection and Extension Constants](file-type-detection-and-extensions.md) - How src/mimetype.rs centralises the extension constants, the shared infer-based magic-byte matchers (CHD, CSO, RVZ, IRD, BPS, IPS, XDELTA, ZSO, RDSK, RIFF) and the ARCHIVE_EXTENSIONS / PS3_EXTENSIONS / NON_ORIGINAL_EXTENSIONS groups that import, conversion, export and integrity-check dispatch all read, and how a CHD's own type is resolved by parsing the container with chd-rs rather than by sniffing.
- [Format Modules and Their Crate Backends](format-modules-and-format-crates.md) - The per-format modules in src/ (archive, chd, ctrtool, rvz, wbfs, iso, gdi, nsz, xso, xdelta, xps, crc32), the wrapper-plus-As*/To* pattern they share, that all run in-process on their format crates except CIA which shells out to ctrtool, and the archive and disc-image behaviours worth not breaking.
- [Romfile Trait System](romfile-trait-system.md) - How CommonRomfile plus the FromPath, CommonFile, GetRomfile, As*/To*, Size, HashAndSize, HeaderedHashAndSize, Check, Playlist and Persist traits abstract over every ROM file format, how format modules such as src/chd.rs plug into them across the spawn_blocking boundary, and the tmp-directory contract around conversion and verification.
