- [The CommonRomfile Trait System](romfile-trait-system.md) - How CommonRomfile plus the FromPath, CommonFile, GetRomfile, As*/To*, Size, HashAndSize, HeaderedHashAndSize, Check, Playlist and Persist traits abstract over every ROM file format, how format modules plug into them, and the tmp-directory contract around conversion and verification.
