# 0.1.0

## Changes

- The metadata writes as RFC 8949 §4.2.1 core deterministic CBOR, every map's keys shortest first and then bytewise, where they were written in declaration order: what a reader takes back is unchanged, keys of any order still read, and once the metadata's bytes must be one encoding — for signing, or for two writers agreeing — there now is one; SPEC.md §7 says so, `format.rs` holds a test that a field declared out of order fails, and a `compat` vector keeps the old order readable

## Features

- Added `Reader::title_files` and `Reader::ticket`: a Wii U eShop title's files, each its name and where it lies in the byte view, and its ticket, so a reader gives a file of the title without writing the folder out
- Added `Index` among the exports, a store's index as a value: `Index::load` reads one from a store, and its `stats` and `shares` answer from what is already read what `store_stats` and `shares` answer from the store's path, so a caller that asks the same store many times parses it once
- Added `store_stats`, what a store's tricks get it and what they cost it: its distinct chunks and what they weigh where they lie, the bytes its dedup kept from being stored twice, and the footers of parity over its segments, all read off its index alone, no data
- Added `SPEC.md`, ORE 1.0's specification, its codecs `xa`, `vag` and `str` among it, and its conformance vectors in `spec/vectors`: every media class, record kind and codec, a store and its thin OREs, what a later writer adds and what a reader refuses
- Added `shares`, the `Sizes::share` of many OREs of one store, its index loaded once for them all
- Added 3DS card images, `3ds` among `CARTRIDGE_PLATFORMS`: a ROM whose NCSD header says it is no larger than the card, a trimmed one too, up to 4 GiB; `default_codec` gives Zstandard for one, read as a disc is rather than whole
- Added Wii U eShop titles, games, updates and DLC as NUS folders, CDN or downloader named: their files end to end, each content stored decrypted by its title key when the folder holds its ticket, kept as it is otherwise, the ticket kept beside them as an SBI is beside a cue sheet, each file's hashes recorded as a DAT lists them; decompressed back to the folder, file for file. `is_title_folder` tells such a folder, and orers compresses one
- Added Wii U discs, from a WUD, WUX or ISO, laid out as wux-rs lays out a lossless WUA: each partition's regions stored decrypted, by the disc key and by their titles' keys, the system titles of the update partition included, and encrypted back on read, every 64 KiB of a chain's IV kept so a read starts anywhere; the free space a partition's mastering fills with the region before it a repeat; each region checked at encode to encrypt back. The disc and title keys are kept in the ORE, so reading needs no keys file; writing needs the disc key and the Wii U common key, and the vWii common key for a vWii title to be decrypted
- Added `Track::flags`, a CD track's cue sheet `FLAGS` as the Q subchannel's control bits, a cue sheet with a flag it does not know refused
- Added `Reader::tracks`, a CD's tracks as its cue sheet and its areas give them, and `Reader::subchannel_q`, the Q subchannel its SBI replaces, an address past its bounds refused
- Added the `write` feature, on by default: without it ore-rs reads OREs and stores and checks them, and leaves out the writer, the CLI and the GameCube and Wii disc model ported from Dolphin, and the reader so built is MIT OR Apache-2.0 but for the CD sector model it embeds, BSD-3-Clause, each file's license in an SPDX header and its text packaged
- Added ORE compression of GameCube and Wii discs, bit-exact: Wii partitions stored decrypted and without their hashes, those recomputing would not give back kept as exceptions, and each group checked to encrypt back to what the disc holds; padding stored as its seed and long runs of zeros as their length; the rest cut into MinCDC chunks of 8 to 24 KiB, each stored once, and compressed in groups of 1 MiB (`stream`) or 4 MiB (`archive`) with Zstandard 19 or LZMA2
- Added the disc's CRC-32, MD5, SHA-1 and BLAKE3, recorded when it is written, so it can be checked against a DAT without decoding it
- Added ORE decompression, which checks the disc against the BLAKE3 it was written with, and `Reader`, which reads an ORE as the disc it holds, at any offset
- Added Wii common keys as runtime input, read from text, a BootMii `keys.bin` or an OTP dump; none ships with the crate, and an ORE keeps its title keys, so reading one needs none
- Added stores: a system's `.ore-store`, holding the chunks of every disc written to it once, in segments under 4 GiB by default, each game a thin ORE naming its chunks there and finding its store as the nearest one up from its folder; chunk ids of 32 bits by default, or 64
- Added Reed-Solomon parity to every store segment, RS(255,239) by default, with a CRC-32C a 4 KiB block, and `check_store`, which checks a store whole, rebuilds what is damaged in place, decodes every group and hashes every chunk, and names the games using what is past repair; a segment is written by one writer and sealed when done, never added to again
- Added `attach` and `detach`, moving a game into a store and out of it, its disc, hashes and metadata as they were, and `compact`, counting again which chunks the games use and copying what is left of mostly dead segments, and of small ones, into new ones
- Added `Reader::sizes`, what a game weighs: its own chunks, its share of those it shares, and about what it would weigh detached; and `Reader::inspect`, a thin ORE's identity without its store
- Added Dreamcast GD-ROMs, from a Redump cue sheet: its tracks after `REM HIGH-DENSITY AREA` addressed from sector 45,000, as the disc holds them, so their sectors are generated as a CD's are; media class 48
- Added PlayStation 2 DVDs, an ISO whose `SYSTEM.CNF` boots a `BOOT2` executable: its runs of zeros stored as their length and the rest cut into MinCDC chunks, deduped and compressed in groups as a GameCube disc's, with no padding looked for
- Added PSP UMDs, an ISO holding `UMD_DATA.BIN` or `PSP_GAME/PARAM.SFO`, cut as a PlayStation 2 DVD is, its audio found and coded the same way
- Added Game Boy, Game Boy Color and Game Boy Advance ROMs, media class 17, recognised by what their console checks in the header, the Game Boy's Nintendo logo and the Game Boy Advance's header checksum: one area cut and deduped as a disc image is, the ROM's file name kept so `orers` writes it back with its extension, and `Reader::file_name` giving it
- Added DS, N64 and Mega Drive ROMs: a DS's by its logo CRC and header CRC-16, up to 1 GiB, an N64's by its first word in any of the three byte orders, a Mega Drive's or a 32X's by its `SEGA` at 0x100
- Added `Options::platform`, `CARTRIDGE_PLATFORMS` and `orers --platform`: a ROM named the ROM of one of 30 cartridges, NES, SNES, PC Engine, Master System and Atari's among them, is taken whole, its header not checked, as a ROM manager vouches for it from its DAT; `Reader::platform` gives it back, and `default_codec` takes it; `cartridge_header_matches` says whether its header agrees
- Added `set_parity` and `orers --parity`: a store's parity changed after it is made, each segment's recorded in the index, now version 2, which still reads version 1's; a segment sealed from then on takes the new parity, and `compact` seals again those sealed otherwise, copying their groups as they are
- Added `fill`, generator 5: a run of one byte not zero, a whole block of it or more, stored as that byte and its length, as zeros are, so a cartridge's 0xFF padding is no longer cut into chunks
- Added `vag` and `pcm`, lossless coders of a PlayStation 2 DVD's audio, found by its `VAGp`, `SShd` and `RIFF WAVE` headers as the disc is hashed and stored as areas of their own after the rest, in 16 KiB chunks from the stream's start: `vag` codes SPU ADPCM blocks as `xa` codes XA, each 4-bit sample against an LMS predictor of the decoded stream; `pcm` puts 16-bit samples, de-interleaved, through FLAC. Areas may now be listed out of disc order, the order their records come, and name a codec of their own
- Added CDs, PlayStation's first: a cue sheet and a BIN a track compressed with each data sector's sync, address, EDC and ECC generated rather than stored, a sector that does not fit the standard kept whole, its audio tracks in FLAC in 8-sector frames, every track's, the cue sheet's and the SBI's hashes recorded under their names; decompressed back to those files, and an SBI beside the cue sheet kept and written back
- Added `xa`, a lossless coder of CD-ROM XA ADPCM: a CD data track's XA audio sectors stored after its other data, each 4-bit sample range coded against an LMS predictor of the decoded channel, 12.7% under Zstandard on a PlayStation library's XA; a group Zstandard does better on is stored with it instead
- Added `str`, a lossless coder of STR video: a CD data track's video sectors stored after its other data, each frame's MDEC bitstream, BS v1 to v3 and Final Fantasy VII's, VIII's and IX's variants, parsed into its codes and range coded against the frame before's, 23.2% under Zstandard on a PlayStation library's video; a frame whose codes do not write back to it byte for byte is coded as its bytes
- Added padding to a group that codes to less than the 1,024th of itself a reader refuses as a decompression bomb, a skippable frame in Zstandard and zeros after the end in the others
- Added `replace_cue`, putting another cue sheet in a CD ORE, its metadata alone written again: it must describe the same tracks, audio and data as they were, at the same frames, and gives the cue sheet's and its tracks' names
- Added folding: a write to a store copies its last segment, when 4 MiB or less and every group of it passes its CRC, into the segment it writes, then drops it, so a ROM added alone no longer leaves a segment and 64 KiB of parity of its own
- Added a store reader's second look: a segment a compaction or a fold removed under a reader that loaded the index before it sends it to the index again, which the writer saved first, so a game being read while its store is written to reads on
- Added `recompress` to `compact` and `--recompress` to `orers`: every segment written again and every group decoded and packed again, the smallest a store gets, for a read and a write of all of it
- Added re-packing to `compact`: the groups of a segment mostly of groups less than half full, as games written a write each leave, are decoded and packed together again, so a store written a game at a time compacts to what one batch would have written; a re-packed chunk keeps its group's codec, audio and data alike, and a group copied whole keeps its place, so chunks stay in the order they were written and a later `recompress` packs them as one batch would have
- Added `StoreBatch`, writing many games to a store as one write: their chunks share its groups and segments and it is committed once, every thin ORE landing then, so a library of small ROMs no longer pays a sealed segment a ROM; `orers --store` writes all its inputs as one batch
- Added `default_codec`, LZMA2 for a cartridge's ROM and Zstandard otherwise, which `orers` takes when `-c` is not given
- Added a progress callback to the library API, reporting input bytes consumed
- Added writing to `<output>.part`, renamed into place once complete, so a failed run leaves an existing output alone and nothing else behind
- Added the `orers` CLI, which compresses ISOs and cue sheets, alone or into a store, decompresses, attaches and detaches OREs, and checks, repairs and compacts stores, as many as given, in oxyROMon's look, refusing to write one input's output over another's; it sits behind the default `cli` feature
- Added progress callbacks to `check_store` and `compact`, adding up to the new `store_size`, and to `StoreBatch::commit`, adding up to the new `StoreBatch::unsealed`, and bars to `orers` checking, compacting and committing a store

## Fixes

- Reading ahead stops at its window past the playhead's group, four groups by default, where it counted only the groups it had yet to decode and walked past the ones held to find that many: every read sent the worker a window further, so a disc played from a share was fetched about as fast as the link carried it
- A Wii U disc's `repeat` area longer than its period, as its free space is, reads in one step, folded onto the period before it, where the reader followed it back one period at a time, a stack frame each: a disc's gigabytes of free space overflowed the stack, which aborted oxyromon's check of a fresh ORE on a tokio worker
- An ORE whose header and metadata name different media classes is refused: the file name was read from the header's class and the outputs from the metadata's, so the two could disagree on what a file is
- A header longer than 192 bytes is read, its groups found after it and the bytes past the 192 skipped, as the spec has it, and kept as they are by a cue sheet's replacement, so a later version growing the header leaves this one able to read its files; and an ORE of a later layout, `ORE2` on, is refused as one, not as a file of another kind
- A cue sheet naming a track not beside it, in a directory below or by way of one, is refused, as `replace_cue` refuses it: the cue sheet is kept as it is and its tracks written beside it, so the set written back would not have read
- A CD track's sector map is decoded as far as it goes and sized by what it holds, no longer allocating for the sectors its ORE claims, so a crafted ORE no longer asks for terabytes
- Decompressing reports its progress a slice at a time, its cores kept busy a few slices ahead of what is written, rather than once every core's slice was decoded
- `orers --parity` with `--store` writes more than one input, rather than waiting forever from the second on the store's lock, which the batch the first opened holds: the store is set up once, before the batch opens
- `orers --store` checks a store given as an input after inputs written to it, rather than waiting forever on its lock, which their open batch holds: what they wrote is committed first
